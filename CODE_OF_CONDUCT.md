# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and the rights of asset owners.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, dox, spam, or use sexualized language or
  imagery.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues,
pull requests, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may also
temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/luckeyfaraday). Reports
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as practical. Project
maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy consistently may be
temporarily or permanently removed from the project team.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project or its community.
